Tell your audio lessons how they went, read your own texts, and get a daily reminder to study.

Audio lessons
- Finishing a lesson now comes with a little celebration.
- After a beginner lesson, say how it was: pace, repetition and length. Zeeguu tells you how your next lesson will change.
- Choose where your lessons take place from a short list of everyday situations, with how many lessons you have had in each, or type your own. After a lesson, Zeeguu also suggests where to go next.
- The button under the player now shows what your lessons are about.

Reading
- Share a short text from another app to Zeeguu, like a message, and tap any word to translate it. Your texts are in Inbox, next to what friends share.
- New Evergreen tab, next to News: articles that stay worth reading, filtered by your topics and keywords.
- Phrases whose words are apart are now numbered, so you can see which words belong together. Their text no longer jumps when translated, and you can ungroup them.

Practice
- In the exercise with three audio options, a wrong pick now tells you which word you picked and what it means.
- A daily study reminder, on the Notifications page.
- Aiki shows how many seconds of study unlock your paused apps.
