Article cards, rebuilt around the photo.

In the feed:
- The photo now comes first on every card. A picture tells you what an article is about before you have to read a headline in a language you are still learning.
- The photo also says where tapping will take you: Read for an article you can read inside Zeeguu, Summary for one that opens a summary first.
- Save sits on the photo, and Hide has moved into the … menu beside the article details, so it stays out of the way until you want it.
- The three feed views — Headlines, Preview and Interactive — now look and behave the same.

Also:
- Summaries no longer get cut off mid-word on wider screens.
