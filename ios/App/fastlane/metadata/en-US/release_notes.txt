A fix for the word-matching exercise, and take your article along when you switch languages.

Practice
- Fixed: the word-matching exercise could get stuck at the end, with no way to continue. The Next button is back, along with Show solution and Report.
- Typed answers in fill-in-the-word exercises now match the look of the revealed answer.

Reading
- Switching language while reading now lets you keep going: Zeeguu can translate the article into your new language, adapted to your level there. Or go to your home page, and the article is saved for later.
