A clearer way around the app, smarter practice, and more reliable translations in your feed.

Getting around
- The bottom bar is now Read, Practice, Listen, Translate and More. My Words and My Activity are under More.
- Settings has its own button in the top bar, next to your profile.
- Settings is organized into five groups, each item with an icon, so it is easier to find what you need.
- Profile and Settings have a back arrow, and you can swipe back.
- The language switcher lists all your languages, the one you practiced most recently first.

Practice
- Now and then a word is tested without hints, to check that you really know it.
- When your exercises are full, Zeeguu tells you that new words are waiting.
- Typing exercises have a Check button next to Enter.
- In translation exercises, an answer that is almost right no longer gives away the solution.
- "Remove from practice" asks whether you already know the word.
- Translate what you hear: the speaker, your answer and Check sit on one row.

Aiki mode
- When you open a paused app, Zeeguu invites you to learn a bit, and shows how many minutes are left until your apps unlock for the rest of the day.
- A new page shows what you can do next: practice your words, continue reading, or listen to today's lesson.
- Aiki mode now lives under Distraction Interception in Settings, with a short note on where the name comes from.

Translations
- The translation menu is calmer and easier to read. Alternatives slide in when they arrive, and are fetched again if the translators were slow.
- Fixed: translating a multi-word expression in an article summary could make some of its words disappear.
- Fixed: a translation you added or removed in the feed could be undone after visiting Settings.

Smaller fixes
- The article preview on the phone is cleaner, with a handle to swipe it away.
- The Feed view options in Settings no longer run off the screen.
- Texts from your teacher keep their blank lines between paragraphs.
- Sending an article to Zeeguu shows one clear message while it is being prepared.
