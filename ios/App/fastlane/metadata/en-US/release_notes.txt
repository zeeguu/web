Sharing articles:
- A shared article now opens for people who don't have Zeeguu. They can read the whole article in their browser, tap up to 10 words for a translation, and then create an account or get the app.
- Article links are short and look the same whether you copy them from the address bar or press Share.
- Tapping a shared Zeeguu link opens the article straight in the app.
- An article that was already simplified no longer asks to be adapted again when it is opened from a shared link.

Also:
- New accounts that haven't verified their email yet no longer see a misleading "can't reach the server" message, and the verify-email screen shows up properly again.
- The About page can now be opened while you are logged in.
