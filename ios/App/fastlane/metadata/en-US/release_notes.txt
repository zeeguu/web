- New Feed View options: choose how your feed looks — Headlines (title + image), Preview (adds a short summary), or Interactive (tappable words right in the feed). Change it in Settings, under Feed preferences, in the View section.
- Tap any article for an interactive preview: translate and listen to words in the title and summary before you open it.
- Share articles with friends, and find what others send you in a new "Shared with you" inbox.
- Leaderboards: friends and classmates now live in one place, with a simple switch between them.

- Settings: a new page to manage your email notifications.
