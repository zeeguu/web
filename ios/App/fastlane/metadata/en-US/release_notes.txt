Hear the variety of the language you are actually learning.

Audio lessons:
- Language Settings now asks which variety of your language should be read to you. A Brazilian Portuguese learner has until now been hearing European Portuguese; you can say which one you want, and your daily lesson is voiced that way.
- The question is only asked for languages whose varieties have a voice, so most learners see nothing new.
- If you have never chosen, the variety you are already hearing is shown as selected - the screen tells you what you get instead of leaving it blank.

Signing up:
- The two regional questions - which country's news you read, and which variety is spoken to you - are now asked together, on a step of their own. They used to be split across two screens, where one was easily mistaken for the other.
- That step is skipped entirely when the language you are learning has no varieties to ask about.
- A variety picked while signing up is now actually saved. Before, you could choose Belgium, finish signing up, and find something else selected in Settings.

Also:
- Every label on the language form is now the learner speaking: "I want news from", "I want audio lessons spoken in".
- The language forms now put visible space between groups of questions, instead of reading as one long list.
