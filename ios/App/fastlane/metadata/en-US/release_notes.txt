Sharing

Audio

- The player stays in sync when you come back to the app.

History

For teachers
- A new Activity tab for each student: what they read and practised, the words they looked up, and the original article.
- Open a text from a student's activity and see their translations in place.
- A student's Words tab now shows their words the way they see them.
- Sort the class list by name or by how active students are, and search for a student.
- Students who likely have two accounts in the same class are flagged and shown side by side.
- Each class card shows when the class was last active.
- Decide whether the students of a class can see each other.
