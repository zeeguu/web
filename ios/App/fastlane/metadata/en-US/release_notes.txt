A round of small fixes, mostly about readability.

- Text on orange buttons and tags is now dark instead of white, so it is much easier to read: the topic label in the feed, interest tags, the translate button, the keyboard in exercises and more.
- In dark mode, translated words in the reader are marked in a softer orange, so a sentence still reads as one line instead of being split in two.
- Opening an article from a link now picks up its level correctly, and the level bars show the right amount instead of always appearing full.
- Removed the "hide this topic" button from video cards. It never actually saved anything.
- Bulgarian now shows its name in the language lists instead of a blank entry.
