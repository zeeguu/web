Start a language from zero, and fairer exercises.

Starting from zero
- A new level for learners who have never studied the language: "Don't know anything".
- At this level your audio lessons start from the very beginning, and your exercises are listening only: hear a word and choose what it means, with nothing to type.
- When you pick a daily situation, you can say whether you speak as a man, a woman, or either, and the lesson's dialogue is written for you.
- Instead of a news feed you cannot read yet, the home page points you to the audio lessons. The feed comes back at A1.

Exercises
- In cloze exercises, if you type another word that also fits the sentence, it is checked before being marked wrong.
- When you type a translation that differs from the saved one, it is checked too, and correct answers count.
- "to look up" and "look up" are now the same answer.
- After an answer, a short line tells you what you typed and how it compares with your saved translation.
- Pressing Enter in an answer field no longer types the first letter of the on-screen keyboard.
- Translations now match the capitalisation of the word they sit over.

Account
- To change your email, you now get a code at the new address to confirm it.
