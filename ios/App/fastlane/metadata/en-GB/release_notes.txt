Aiki mode: pause distracting apps, and earn breaks by learning.

Aiki mode
- New in Settings: choose the apps that distract you, and Zeeguu pauses them for you.
- Each minute you learn in Zeeguu unlocks a few minutes in your paused apps. Reach your daily goal and they stay open for the rest of the day.
- A paused app shows a Zeeguu screen with your progress: today's minutes, one block per minute.

Switching languages
- The menu now shows the flag of the language you are learning.
- When you switch, a spinner shows on the flag until the change is saved, and the app only moves to the new language once it is.
- Right after a switch, exercises now pick words in the new language and read them in the right voice.

Exercises
- The exercise stays in view while the keyboard is open.
- When you have no words left to practise, the message now counts only the words in the language you are learning.

Shared articles
- A shared article can now be translated into any language Zeeguu supports.
