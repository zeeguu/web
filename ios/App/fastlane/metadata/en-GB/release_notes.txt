Smarter exercises, a new profile page, and more for teachers.

Exercises
- Explain is now available in exercises too.
- When typing a translation, any listed form of the word counts as right.
- In typed cloze exercises, a wrong answer is followed by its correction read aloud.
- Multi-word answers are blanked correctly in sentences that start mid-way.
- Example sentences show when there are more to see, with a swipe hint on touch screens.
- Words you already know well move faster through the level bar, with a celebration when they are learned.

Feed and reading
- A story covered by several outlets now lists them all as equals.
- Shared summaries let you switch level and sign up at the level you chose.

Profile and settings
- Settings now sit behind your profile, and you edit your avatar on the profile page.
- Daily lesson settings are shown even before your first lesson arrives.
- Every learner can now delete a daily lesson.

History
- Words from a browsing session appear under the article they came from.
- Words you have practised since are marked.

For teachers
- A new page for each class text shows the words the class looked up in it.
- Reading sessions show a focus strip of when the student was active.
- A compact student table and a cleaner class page.
- Add Text opens the editor straight away.
- Opening a student's text no longer shows a blank page.
