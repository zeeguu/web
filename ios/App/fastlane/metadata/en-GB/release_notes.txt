An easier translation menu and a fix for typing exercises.

Translations
- Alternative translations now appear at reading size, and each one is a comfortable tap target on your phone.
- The translation menu is simpler: it shows plain Alternatives, without the names of the translators.

Practice
- Fixed: after a wrong answer in a fill-in-the-word typing exercise, you could get stuck with no way to continue. The exercise now ends and shows the answer.
- Pressing Enter with nothing typed no longer counts as a wrong answer.
